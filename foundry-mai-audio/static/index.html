What this demo does not claim.

Microsoft reports words appearing as early as 320 ms and Flash generating 45 seconds of audio in 150 ms in its launch material. Those are reported examples, not guarantees or measurements from this page. Our timer measures request-to-complete audio; transcription timing starts at the first audio chunk, not a word boundary.

Voice cloning requires Limited Access approval and recorded consent. It is intentionally absent. This is a caption and synthesis studio, not a full reasoning voice agent.
